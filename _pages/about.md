I am a PhD candidate in [Computer Science at Duke University](https://cs.duke.edu/), advised by [Kartik Nayak](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~kartik/) at Duke and [Saba Eskandarian](https://www.cs.unc.edu/~saba/) at UNC–Chapel Hill.

I study **applied cryptography, decentralized systems, and economic security**. My goal is to make decentralized infrastructure private, resilient, and economically robust. My recent work covers privacy-preserving inclusion lists and reputation-based peer-to-peer systems; I am also exploring mechanism design and the economic security of DeFi.

My collaborations extend to AI evaluation and data-driven optimization. Previously, I earned a master's degree in Mathematics from [UW–Madison](https://math.wisc.edu/) in 2023 and a bachelor's degree in Mathematics, with a minor in French, from the Wen-Tsun Wu Honors Class at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://math.sjtu.edu.cn/) in 2022.
